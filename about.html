Who We Are

Business Connections That Create Value

Niaz Net Tehran is a strategic distribution and international business development company focused on connecting manufacturers, healthcare businesses, established brands and emerging technologies with new market opportunities.

We work across pharmaceuticals, healthcare, sports nutrition, infant nutrition, nutraceuticals, medical devices and related industries, helping businesses develop reliable commercial relationships and sustainable routes to market.

We believe successful international business is built on trust, market knowledge, strong partnerships and consistent execution. Our approach combines strategic sourcing, business development, commercial coordination and market-entry support to help our partners navigate complex markets and identify opportunities for long-term growth.

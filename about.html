Our Role in the Value Chain

NIAZ NET works at the intersection of international trade, pharmaceutical and healthcare industries, nutrition and business development. We identify commercial opportunities, connect the right parties and coordinate the steps needed to move qualified opportunities forward.

What We Do

Four focused business pathways — one connected international network.

01 / NIAZ NET

Global Brands & Iranian Manufacturers

Connecting international brands and technology owners with suitable Iranian manufacturers for local production, contract manufacturing and long-term industrial cooperation.

02 / NIAZ NET

Iranian Products to Global Buyers

Introducing qualified Iranian-made products to international wholesalers, distributors and commercial partners, and supporting export-market development.

03 / NIAZ NET

Strategic Sourcing from China & India

Sourcing pharmaceutical APIs, excipients and specialized raw materials from qualified suppliers in China, India and other markets for the Iranian market.

04 / NIAZ NET

Iranian Products & Materials Worldwide

Developing international market opportunities for Iranian pharmaceutical, healthcare, nutrition products and selected raw materials.

Who We Are

Established in 1997 (1376 SH), Niaz Net Tehran supports international business development, strategic distribution, sourcing and commercial partnerships across selected global markets.

Our Network

Our business network connects with distributors, wholesalers and commercial partners across the Middle East, Africa, CIS, Europe, Latin America, Türkiye and Spain. We coordinate introductions and market-development opportunities based on each project’s requirements.

What We Do

We support brand registration and representation, product and raw-material sourcing, market entry, distribution partnerships, regulatory-document coordination, technology transfer and production-line discussions.

Our Industries & Capabilities

Our areas of focus include pharmaceuticals and APIs, sports nutrition and supplements, vitamins and nutraceuticals, infant nutrition, aesthetic medicine such as dermal fillers and botulinum toxin, pet food and animal nutrition, specialty ingredients, molecules and formulations.

We work to align manufacturers and brand owners with appropriate commercial partners, help organize sourcing and documentation discussions, and coordinate opportunities for local production or technology transfer. Product registration, licensing, manufacturing feasibility and regulatory approvals depend on the product and destination market and must be confirmed with the relevant authorities and qualified partners.

Our Mission

To build reliable, long-term business relationships that help quality products, technologies and expertise reach suitable markets through structured communication, responsible coordination and practical commercial planning.

Our Vision

To be a trusted international partner for businesses seeking sustainable market expansion, strategic distribution and cross-border cooperation.

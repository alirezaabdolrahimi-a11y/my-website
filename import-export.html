Import & Export Services: commercial coordination

We coordinate import and export opportunities between suppliers, manufacturers, buyers, and distribution partners across international markets. Support may include supplier and buyer communication, quotation collection, commercial-term coordination, document follow-up, and coordination with freight, customs, and logistics parties.

Explore African Market Opportunities

Each transaction is assessed according to the product, origin and destination markets, applicable trade rules, and agreed Incoterms. Customs clearance, permits, payment arrangements, shipping, and compliance are handled with the relevant authorized parties; specific requirements and responsibilities are confirmed before execution.

How we approach this service

We organize the commercial hand-offs among buyers, suppliers and logistics parties, with the product, origin, destination and agreed responsibilities identified early. Customs, permits and shipping arrangements must be confirmed for each transaction.

Information to include in an enquiry

  • Product description, origin and destination country; HS code if already known.
  • Buyer/supplier roles, indicative volume, timing and agreed Incoterms if available.
  • Required certificates, permits, shipping documents and payment or delivery terms to be reviewed by the relevant parties.

Regulatory approvals, import eligibility, quality acceptance, licensing and transaction feasibility must be confirmed for each product and destination market by the responsible parties and qualified professionals. NIAZ NET coordinates commercial communication; it does not replace regulatory authorities, legal advisers or independent quality assessment.

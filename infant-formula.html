Infant Formula & Baby Milk: commercial coordination

Niaz Net Tehran facilitates business development and sourcing discussions for infant formula and baby nutrition products with manufacturers and prospective market partners. We help coordinate product information, commercial terms, supply expectations, and the communication required to assess a potential distribution relationship.

Because infant nutrition is a highly regulated category, product eligibility, labeling, registrations, and import conditions must be reviewed for each destination market. We coordinate the relevant stakeholders so commercial planning and regulatory review can progress in a structured way.

How we approach this service

We treat infant nutrition enquiries as product- and market-specific because age stage, formulation, labelling and import rules can differ substantially. Commercial discussions should progress alongside an appropriate regulatory review.

Information to include in an enquiry

  • Product stage and type, pack size and available product information.
  • Destination market and planned distribution channel.
  • Current quality and regulatory documents, label information and remaining-shelf-life expectations.

Regulatory approvals, import eligibility, quality acceptance, licensing and transaction feasibility must be confirmed for each product and destination market by the responsible parties and qualified professionals. NIAZ NET coordinates commercial communication; it does not replace regulatory authorities, legal advisers or independent quality assessment.

Discuss this service

Send us the product or project details, target market and the type of support required. Our team can review the initial scope and identify the information needed for a useful commercial discussion.

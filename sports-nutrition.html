SPORTS NUTRITION. SMART SOURCING. NEW MARKETS.

Grow your product portfolio with NIAZ NET

With business reach and coordination across IRAN, Türkiye and Spain, NIAZ NET helps companies explore international brand opportunities and coordinate product sourcing for sports nutrition, supplements, and health-oriented food products. We bring commercial conversations, sourcing needs, and market objectives together so your business can move forward with greater clarity.

Sports nutrition & supplement brands

We help businesses identify suitable international sports nutrition brands, connect with brand owners, and explore representation agreements and commercial partnerships for target markets. Each opportunity is assessed around your product portfolio, business goals, and intended market.

Bulk sports powder supply

For manufacturers, packers, and supplement distributors, we coordinate bulk sourcing opportunities for sports powders and performance nutrition products. Product type, technical specifications, order quantities, supplier terms, and documentation needs are reviewed according to each business requirement.

Vitamins, wellness supplements & diet-related foods

We also help businesses explore brand representation and bulk sourcing opportunities for vitamin products, health-oriented supplements, and food products related to diet and healthy living. Potential supply and partnership options are considered according to the product category, supplier capabilities, and target-market requirements.

All brand representation and supply opportunities are subject to the brand owner’s or supplier’s approval, commercial terms, product availability, and applicable regulatory requirements in the destination market.

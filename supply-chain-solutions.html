Supply Chain Solutions: commercial coordination

We coordinate supply-chain communication among buyers, suppliers, manufacturers, freight providers, and other relevant parties. Depending on the project, this may include order and shipment follow-up, documentation tracking, delivery planning, and escalation of outstanding commercial or operational questions.

The exact scope depends on the transaction, product requirements, and the parties’ responsibilities. Shipping, customs, insurance, and handling arrangements are confirmed with the authorized providers and documented before dispatch.

How we approach this service

We clarify responsibilities and information flows among the buyer, supplier, freight provider and other involved parties. Shipping, insurance, customs and handling are performed by the authorized providers under transaction-specific agreements.

Information to include in an enquiry

  • Goods, quantity, origin, destination and requested delivery window.
  • Current order/shipment status, named parties and agreed Incoterms if known.
  • Documents outstanding, handling requirements and the point that needs escalation.

Regulatory approvals, import eligibility, quality acceptance, licensing and transaction feasibility must be confirmed for each product and destination market by the responsible parties and qualified professionals. NIAZ NET coordinates commercial communication; it does not replace regulatory authorities, legal advisers or independent quality assessment.

Discuss this service

Send us the product or project details, target market and the type of support required. Our team can review the initial scope and identify the information needed for a useful commercial discussion.
